Calculator methodology

Every output on MissedCallStack is an estimate derived from inputs you control. If an assumption is wrong for your shop, change it. We do not invent testimonials or promise recovery.

Core formulas

Missed calls / mo = inbound calls × (miss rate ÷ 100)

Monthly revenue at risk = missed calls × (live booking rate ÷ 100) × average ticket

Annual revenue at risk = monthly at risk × 12

Monthly recovered (text-back) = monthly at risk × recovery rate × after-hours weight

where after-hours weight = 0.85 + (after-hours share × 0.15), then capped so recovered ≤ at risk.

The after-hours weight slightly emphasizes shops with more nights/weekend volume (where automation matters more). It does not invent extra revenue beyond the at-risk pool.

Recovery applies to at-risk revenue, not raw missed-call count. That keeps booking rate and ticket explicit.

Break-even jobs

Break-even jobs / mo = (HighLevel plan price + SMS mid-band estimate) ÷ average ticket

Plan prices default to public list: $97 Starter and $297 Unlimited from gohighlevel.com/pricing. Confirm live pricing; annual billing and add-ons differ. SMS mid-band uses ~$0.012 per segment as a planning placeholder (low ~$0.0075, high ~$0.02). Actual HighLevel/carrier usage fees vary.

If SMS volume is blank, we estimate segments ≈ missed calls × 2.

Answering service comparison

When “answering service” is selected:

  • Cost = your entered monthly fee
  • Recovered ≈ missed calls × 50% × live booking rate × ticket
  • Net ≈ recovered − cost

The 50% catch rate is a deliberate simplification for side-by-side display — not industry gospel. Replace it mentally with your vendor’s answer rate if you know it. GHL net uses your text-back preset minus (plan + SMS mid).

What we intentionally exclude

  • Income claims, “make $10k with GHL,” or fabricated case studies
  • Close rates on cold outbound (this tool is inbound-miss focused)
  • ServiceTitan/HCP-specific fee schedules
  • Tax, chargebacks, or no-show rates (you can lower ticket or booking rate to approximate)

Stack recommendation logic

Copy is rules-based: always prefer keeping the selected field CRM for ops; recommend adding HighLevel for marketing/SMS; note native Jobber sync vs. Zapier/Make for other CRMs. It is guidance, not a personalized consultation.
