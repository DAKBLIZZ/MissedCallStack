Review request automation after Jobber jobs (GHL workflow)

Five-star requests should fire when the invoice is paid and the tech is off the driveway — not when a CSR remembers three weeks later. Keep Jobber as the system of record for job close-out. Use HighLevel for the SMS ask, the follow-up, and the CRM trail. This page is the handoff playbook between those two.

Sync reality check: The official Jobber↔HighLevel connection syncs client/contact fields (name, email, phone, address, tags, etc.). It does not sync jobs, notes, or attachments — see what syncs. “Job completed” is therefore a process you design (tag, form, Zap/Make, or CSR checkbox), not a magic toggle. SMS usage and A2P rules still apply — A2P + SMS costs.

Why Jobber stays the ops system

Close the job in Jobber

  • Tech marks complete / visits done
  • Invoice sent and (ideally) paid
  • Internal notes and photos stay on the property

Techs should not open a marketing CRM to “finish” a call.

Ask for the review in HighLevel

  • Short SMS with Google / Facebook / BBB link
  • One polite bump, then stop
  • Tag review-asked / review-left for reporting

Same LC Phone number and A2P campaign you use for text-back.

Recommended workflow (contractor-sized)

  1. Decide the review policy on paper

    Write four rules the owner and CSR both sign:

    • When: e.g. same evening after paid invoice, or next morning if the job finished after 7 p.m.
    • Who gets skipped: callbacks still open, warranty disputes, “do not SMS,” STOP’d contacts, commercial POs that ban solicitation.
    • Which link: one primary (usually Google Business Profile). Do not shotgun three platforms in one text.
    • How many nudges: one ask + one bump max. More feels like spam and burns segments.

  2. Keep close-out in Jobber

    Train techs/CSR: job → invoice → payment (or clear exception) before any review ask. If the homeowner is unhappy on-site, fix the work first — automation should never outrun the callback list.

  3. Choose a handoff into HighLevel

    Because job status does not sync, pick one path your shop will actually maintain:

    • CSR tag (simplest): After Jobber close, CSR opens the matched GHL contact (or creates it) and applies tag job-closed-ask-review. Workflow starts on tag added.
    • Jobber custom field / checklist + weekly batch: Office pulls yesterday’s completed jobs and tags contacts in bulk — ugly but reliable for smaller shops.
    • Middleware (Zapier / Make / etc.): Jobber “job closed” or “invoice paid” event → find/create GHL contact → add tag. Only worth it if volume makes CSR tagging a bottleneck. Budget middleware separately; it is not free HighLevel magic.

    Contact field sync helps matching once the Jobber client exists in GHL — it does not replace the close-out trigger.

  4. Build the GHL review-request workflow

    Typical HighLevel pattern:

    • Trigger: tag job-closed-ask-review added (or inbound webhook if you use middleware).
    • Wait: 2–6 hours (or until next morning) so the text is not competing with “tech still in driveway.”
    • If/else skip: do-not-sms, review-asked in last 90 days, open dispute tag, missing mobile, or DND hours.
    • Send SMS (one segment if you can manage it).
    • Tag review-asked; notify CSR channel optionally.
    • Wait 3–4 days → if no review-left and no STOP, send one shorter bump → then end.

  5. Write short, A2P-safe SMS copy

    Example ask (edit hard):

    Hi {{contact.first_name}} — thanks for choosing {{contact.company_name}} today. If we earned it, a quick Google review helps other homeowners: {{review_link}}. Reply STOP to opt out.

    Name the company. One link. No “guaranteed 5 stars.” No emoji pile that blows Unicode segment limits. Align sample copy with your A2P campaign registration.

    Example bump:

    Quick reminder from {{contact.company_name}} — review link if you have 30 seconds: {{review_link}}. Thanks again. STOP to opt out.

  6. QA for two weeks before peak season

    • Run five real closed jobs through the handoff (tag or middleware).
    • Confirm timing, STOP, and that unhappy-job tags never get the ask.
    • Check segment count and wallet impact — see SMS cost notes.
    • Verify Google link opens on iOS/Android and points at the correct GBP location.

What not to automate

  • Asking during an open callback: If Jobber still has a follow-up visit, wait.
  • Incentivized “leave 5 stars for a gift card” schemes: Platform rules and local advertising law can bite; this page will not coach that.
  • Fake or coached testimonials: Do not script star ratings. Ask for honest feedback; route complaints to a human.
  • Blasting the whole Jobber client list: Review asks belong on recent completed work with consent-ready SMS — not a nostalgia campaign to 2019 filter-change customers.

FAQ

Can HighLevel detect “job completed” in Jobber automatically?

Not via the native sync’s job objects — those do not sync. You need a tag, form, or middleware event. Treat “automatic” as “automatic after our handoff rule fires.”

Should techs send the review link from their personal phones?

Inconsistent, untracked, and hard on A2P brand consistency. Prefer one company LC Phone number and a logged GHL workflow. Techs can still mention reviews verbally; the text should come from the shop system.

Email instead of SMS?

Fine as a supplement for customers without mobile consent. SMS wins on open rate for residential HVAC/plumbing — just stay inside segment budget and consent rules.

Will this guarantee more Google reviews?

No. Automation only improves consistency of the ask. Reviews still depend on job quality, timing, and the customer’s willingness. Anything else is marketing theater.
