Missed-call text-back setup in GHL for HVAC

When a homeowner’s AC dies at 7:40 p.m., the shop that texts back in under two minutes usually wins the slot — even if the truck cannot roll until morning. This is a practical GHL setup for HVAC (plumbing/electrical can clone it), written for owners who already live in Jobber.

Compliance first: Register A2P / brand as required for your country, honor opt-outs, and do not text people who have not consented where the law requires it. This is not legal advice. Carrier filtering will nuke “BUY NOW” spam copy — write like a human dispatcher.

What you are building

A HighLevel workflow that fires when a call to your tracking or main line is missed (or goes to voicemail), sends a short SMS with a clear next step (reply or book), notifies your on-call person, and creates/updates a contact your office can push into Jobber without retyping the phone number.

  1. Inventory numbers and after-hours rules

    List every public number: Google Business Profile, LSA, website click-to-call, truck magnets, yard signs. Decide which numbers ring the office vs. on-call. Write the after-hours policy in one sentence your CSR and techs agree on — e.g. “Emergency heat/AC diagnosis texts get a same-evening callback; non-urgent books next open slot.”

  2. Put a HighLevel number in the path (cleanly)

    In GHL, provision a local number (usage fees apply — see HighLevel pricing/usage docs). Forward your marketing numbers to it, or replace website CTAs gradually. Keep the Jobber/office DID for known customers if you want — many shops dual-path: ads → GHL, old customers → office. Document the diagram on one page so nobody “fixes” forwarding at 2 a.m.

  3. Build the missed-call workflow

    Typical pattern inside HighLevel:

    • Trigger: inbound call missed / voicemail received on the GHL number (use the call events your sub-account supports).
    • Wait 30–90 seconds so a caller who immediately redials is not double-texted.
    • If/else: skip SMS if contact has tag do-not-sms or recent outbound within 10 minutes.
    • Send SMS (template below).
    • Create opportunity in a “Missed Call Recovery” pipeline stage.
    • Notify on-call via GHL app / SMS to the manager’s cell.
    • Follow-up: if no reply in 15–30 minutes during business hours, second softer SMS or CSR task; evenings, one follow-up next morning only.

  4. Write SMS copy that sounds like your shop

    Example (edit hard):

    Hi, this is {{contact.company_name}} — we just missed your call. Reply YES for a callback, or book a slot: {{booking_link}}. For no-heat / no-cool emergencies, reply URGENT. Reply STOP to opt out.

    Keep it under ~320 characters when you can. Name the trade. Never promise “tech in 20 minutes” unless you mean it in February.

  5. Hand off into Jobber (ops stays ops)

    Once the customer replies or books:

    • CSR confirms address, equipment age, and urgency on the phone or in thread.
    • Create/update the Jobber client/request — if you use the official Jobber↔HighLevel sync, contact fields can flow; jobs still get built in Jobber (what syncs).
    • Tag the GHL contact booked / estimate-set so the workflow stops nudging.

  6. QA for seven days

    • Call your own ads number from a personal cell three times (answered, missed, after-hours).
    • Confirm SMS arrives, links work on iPhone and Android, STOP works.
    • Check duplicate contacts — merge ruthlessly.
    • Log: missed calls, texts sent, replies, jobs booked. If reply rate is near zero, your copy or number reputation is the problem — not “SMS doesn’t work.”

Plumbing & electrical variants

Plumbing: emphasize burst/slab leak vs. non-urgent drain — route URGENT differently. Electrical: never coach DIY on live panels in SMS; push to on-call. Same workflow skeleton; change urgency keywords and booking calendars (service vs. estimate).

Where answering services still fit

Text-back is not a full replacement for a live answering service on every shop. High-ticket emergency trades in dense metros sometimes keep a hybrid: answering service for live triage + GHL for SMS, voicemail drops, and daytime overflow.
