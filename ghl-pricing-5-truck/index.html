GoHighLevel pricing for a 5-truck shop: real all-in cost

If you run five trucks on Jobber, you do not need an agency SaaS pitch. You need a clear line between platform list price, usage, and what still belongs in Jobber — then a break-even job count you can argue with on Monday morning.

Pricing source: Public HighLevel list prices on gohighlevel.com/pricing (as of our research pass): Starter $97/mo, Unlimited $297/mo, Agency Pro $497/mo, with a 14-day trial advertised. Annual billing is listed cheaper per month. SMS, phone numbers, email sends, and AI features carry usage-based charges. Always re-check the live page before you budget.

For almost every five-truck HVAC, plumbing, or electrical shop we write for: Starter is the default. Unlimited is an agency/multi-location decision, not a “more trucks need more GHL” rule. Trucks live in Jobber; contacts and SMS live in HighLevel.

What you should still pay Jobber for

Keep in Jobber

  • Requests → quotes → jobs for five trucks
  • Dispatch, routes, tech mobile app
  • Job costing, timesheets, invoices
  • Property history techs need on-site

Add in HighLevel

  • Missed-call text-back & after-hours SMS
  • Speed-to-lead from ads / LSA / web forms
  • Pipeline for estimate → sold → nurture
  • Review asks & seasonal campaigns

Do not budget HighLevel as a Jobber replacement. The honest stack is Jobber for the truck and invoice, HighLevel for the phone and follow-up. See what the official sync covers (client fields yes; jobs/notes/attachments no).

Break-even jobs (labeled math)

Using a $450 average ticket and a ~$150 all-in Starter+SMS mid-band:

  • Break-even jobs / mo ≈ $150 ÷ $450 = 0.33 jobs — less than one average ticket covers the software mid-band.
  • That does not mean the software “pays for itself.” Someone still has to answer replies, book work into Jobber, and keep A2P compliance clean.
  • Compare against answering-service invoices on the GHL vs answering page if nights are brutal.

Costs shops forget (and how to avoid them)

  • Double-paying for the same minute: Full answering service + GHL SMS on the same after-hours pool without a hybrid plan. Decide which channel owns first response.
  • Agency Pro envy: $497 SaaS Mode does nothing for five trucks that a $97 Starter workflow cannot.
  • AI toggled “on” by default curiosity: Test Conversation/Voice AI in a sandbox week with a spend cap; do not leave it unsupervised on emergency lines.
  • Long SMS novels: One missed-call text that spans three segments triples usage for no booking gain. Write like a dispatcher.
  • Rebuilding ops in GHL: Custom fields and pipelines for marketing are fine; forcing techs off Jobber is how projects die.

FAQ

Should a 5-truck shop buy Unlimited for “unlimited contacts”?

No — contacts and users are unlimited on Starter too per HighLevel’s pricing FAQ. Unlimited’s headline difference is unlimited sub-accounts (and related agency features), which a single shop rarely needs.

Is the 14-day trial enough to prove miss recovery?

Enough to wire forwarding, send test SMS, and measure reply rate on real missed calls — not enough to declare seasonal ROI. Treat trial week as QA, then judge over a full billing cycle with the calculator’s conservative preset.

Where do after-hours routing costs show up?

Same SMS/phone usage line items. Routing logic is configuration time, not a separate HighLevel SKU. See after-hours HVAC routing with GHL.
