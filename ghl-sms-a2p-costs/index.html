A2P 10DLC + SMS costs in GoHighLevel for contractors (no surprises)

The $97 Starter line on HighLevel pricing is not your SMS bill. Missed-call text-back, review asks, and after-hours pings all ride A2P registration, monthly campaign fees, and per-segment usage. This page is the contractor version of that stack — Jobber still runs the truck; HighLevel owns the text.

Source & caveat: Figures summarized from HighLevel’s public article A2P 10DLC Messaging Fees (docs note rates as of Aug 1, 2025 for US). TCR, Twilio, and carriers can change prices. Verify in your Trust Center before you budget or submit. Not an invoice.

The three cost buckets (memorize these)

1. One-time registration

Brand + first campaign + Fast Track, billed as one Trust Center bundle when you register LC Phone for U.S. 10DLC.

2. Monthly campaign fee

Recurring TCR fee while the campaign stays active. Use-case dependent. Separate from Starter/Unlimited platform price.

3. Per-segment SMS (LC Phone + carrier)

Base LC Phone SMS rate plus carrier surcharges per segment. Long copy = more segments. Inbound replies can bill too. This is the line item that scales with every missed-call text and review ask.

HighLevel states these A2P fees are passthrough with no HighLevel markup. Platform SaaS (Starter $97, etc.) is separate — see 5-truck all-in pricing.

Per-segment SMS: what a missed-call text actually costs

From the same HighLevel fees article (US LC Phone passthrough examples):

  • SMS outbound / inbound base: $0.0079 per segment
  • Carrier surcharges (examples): often ~$0.003 outbound on major carriers (AT&T / T-Mobile / Verizon listed; US Cellular higher) — confirm live tables
  • Segment math: ~160 GSM-7 characters ≈ 1 segment; longer texts and emojis split into more segments and multiply the bill

Illustrative shop math (labeled estimate only):

Assume ~48 missed calls / month, one short text-back (~1 segment) + reply on half of them (~24 inbound segments), all-in ~$0.011 per segment (base + typical carrier — your mix will differ):

  • Outbound: 48 × ~$0.011 ≈ ~$0.53
  • Inbound replies: 24 × ~$0.011 ≈ ~$0.26
  • Add a one-segment review ask on 80 closed jobs: 80 × ~$0.011 ≈ ~$0.88

SMS usage for a disciplined five-truck shop is often tens of dollars, not hundreds — until someone ships three-segment novels, blasts nurture to the whole list, or leaves MMS on by accident. The surprise is almost always copy length + blast volume, not the A2P one-time fee.

How contractors fail A2P (and burn money)

  • Website / privacy / sample SMS mismatch: Campaign samples must match what you actually send from the missed-call and review workflows.
  • Skipping STOP / consent language: Carriers and TCR expect opt-out and clear brand identity.
  • Going live on ads numbers before approval: Peak-season filtering feels like “SMS is broken.”
  • Multi-segment hero copy: One after-hours essay × every miss × every night = real wallet drain. Write like a dispatcher — see text-back setup and after-hours routing.
  • MMS “logo in every text”: MMS rates are higher than SMS on the published table. Logos rarely book more diagnostics.

Contractor checklist before you flip text-back on

  1. Register brand + campaign in Trust Center

    Use legal business name, EIN when you have one, and sample messages that match production copy. Budget the one-time bundle + first month’s campaign fee.

  2. Link the LC Phone number to the approved campaign

    Unlinked numbers are how shops “registered A2P” and still get filtered.

  3. Cap segments in every template

    Aim for one segment on missed-call and review texts. Count characters before you publish the workflow.

  4. QA with a personal cell

    Miss a call, reply STOP, confirm inbound bills as expected, and watch the usage wallet for a week before peak season.

FAQ

Is A2P a HighLevel upsell SKU?

No. It is industry registration for U.S. local business SMS. HighLevel documents passthrough TCR / Twilio / carrier fees. You would face similar requirements on other LC-Phone-class stacks.

Can I skip A2P and only use an answering service?

Yes as a business choice — but then you are back to per-minute live answer economics. Compare on GHL vs answering service. Many shops run hybrid: live answer for true voice triage + registered GHL SMS for missed marketing calls.

Will A2P fees show on the same invoice as Starter?

Expect platform subscription and usage/Trust Center charges as separate line items in practice. Read your HighLevel billing / wallet screens; do not assume “$97 includes texts.”
