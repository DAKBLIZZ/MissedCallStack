GoHighLevel + Jobber: what syncs, what doesn’t

If you run HVAC, plumbing, or electrical on Jobber, the right move is almost never “rip Jobber out.” It is: keep Jobber for the truck and the invoice — add HighLevel for the phone, SMS, and follow-up that Jobber was never meant to own alone.

This page summarizes public HighLevel documentation as of our research pass. Always re-check the live help article before you change production data: How to Integrate Jobber with HighLevel and the product post HighLevel + Jobber: Smarter service businesses, connected.

The stack split that actually works

Keep in Jobber

  • Requests → quotes → jobs
  • Dispatch, routes, tech mobile app
  • Job costing, timesheets, invoices
  • Client properties & visit history that techs need on-site

Add in HighLevel

  • Missed-call text-back & after-hours SMS
  • Speed-to-lead workflows from Google/LSA/Facebook
  • Pipeline for “estimate set → sold → nurture”
  • Review asks, seasonal campaigns, booking calendars

HighLevel’s own framing matches this: Jobber for day-to-day jobs; HighLevel for sales/marketing automation — with client/contact sync so you are not re-typing every name. That is the honest pitch. Not “all-in-one replaces everything.”

What the official integration syncs

Per HighLevel’s help center FAQ on field coverage, the two-way client/contact sync includes:

Synced (per HighLevel docs)Not synced (per HighLevel docs)
  • Yes First / last name
  • Yes Email, phone
  • Yes Address
  • Yes Lead source
  • Yes Jobber client URL
  • Yes Jobber created date
  • Yes Tags
  • No Custom fields on Jobber
  • No Notes
  • No Attachments

Jobs, invoices, line items, and dispatch remain Jobber’s job. Plan your CRM custom fields in HighLevel if marketing needs them.

Matching, history, and agency gotchas

  • Matching: HighLevel keeps the Jobber client’s unique ID, so sync survives email/phone edits (per their FAQ).
  • Historic import: You pick a start date once when initiating sync. You cannot later change that start date and re-run history from a different point.
  • Throughput: Docs cite roughly 2,500 records per 5 minutes for the Jobber side — large books take time.
  • One Jobber ↔ one sub-account: You cannot hang multiple HighLevel sub-accounts off a single Jobber account.
  • Agency install: The Jobber app is installed by Agency Admins, then connected inside each sub-account. A snapshot with workflows/templates can be pushed on deploy.
  • Stopping sync: HighLevel states there is no pause — disconnect via Jobber’s Connected Apps (LeadConnector) if you need to stop without gutting Jobber workflows.

Practical shop workflow (HVAC example)

  1. Lead hits HighLevel

    Missed call, form, or LSA webhook creates/updates a contact. Workflow sends the text-back and offers a booking link or CSR callback queue.

  2. Contact syncs to Jobber as a client

    Once connected, new/updated clients flow so office staff are not double-entering phones and addresses. Verify tags and lead source mapping on day one.

  3. Job lives in Jobber

    CSR or dispatcher builds the request/job in Jobber, assigns the tech, and invoices there. Techs should not be asked to live inside HighLevel on the truck.

  4. Status back to marketing (manual or light automation)

    Because notes/jobs do not sync, use tags, a simple “Sold / Lost / Maintenance” pipeline stage in GHL, or a weekly CSV for closed-won so seasonal campaigns stay honest.

Housecall Pro & ServiceTitan shops

Native Jobber integration is the cleanest path documented by HighLevel today. If you are on Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan, treat GHL the same way strategically (ops CRM stays; GHL owns SMS/marketing), but plan on Zapier/Make, native HCP/ST marketing tools, or scheduled exports until you validate a connector. Do not pretend the Jobber field list applies to those CRMs.

Pricing context (not a sales pitch)
